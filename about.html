Our Story

Mexxa Pharmacy started with a simple goal: to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone. From our humble beginnings, we have grown into a trusted name with multiple branches, serving thousands of happy customers across Kampala.

We believe that good health should never be out of reach. That is why we are committed to offering genuine medicines, expert advice and excellent customer care every day.

Licensed & Regulated Both Mexxa Pharmacy branches are licensed by the National Drug Authority (NDA) of Uganda and operate in accordance with all pharmacy standards and regulations.
